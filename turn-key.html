We are specialized in providing consultation services for industrial and manufacturing turn-key projects. With our expertise in factory layout design, machinery and toolkit selection, planning, production, and quality assurance, we can help businesses optimize their operations and achieve their production goals efficiently. With years of experience in the industrial and manufacturing sectors, our team of experts has helped numerous businesses successfully set up and streamline their operations. We understand the complexities and challenges involved in starting a new project or expanding an existing one, and we are committed to providing comprehensive and customized solutions to meet our clients’ specific needs.

Factory Layout Design

We can help design an efficient and optimized layout for your factory that maximizes space, streamlines workflow, and enhances productivity.

A well-planned layout is the foundation of a successful industrial project. Whether you are setting up a new facility or expanding an existing one, we design the physical flow of your operation around your products, processes, and growth plans.

  • Analysis of your products, production processes, and capacity targets to define space and equipment requirements.
  • Efficient arrangement of production lines, storage, and work areas to streamline the movement of materials and people.
  • Layouts that support quality control, safety, and compliance with industry standards.
  • Flexible designs that leave room for future expansion and changes in production.

The result is a factory that makes the best use of its space, reduces waste and bottlenecks, and is ready to deliver your production goals efficiently from day one.

Machinery & Toolkit Selection

Our experts will assist you in choosing the right machinery and tools for your production needs, considering factors such as capacity, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness.

The right equipment is one of the most important investments in any industrial project. We help you choose machinery and tools that fit your products, your target output, and your budget, so that you invest in what you actually need and avoid costly over- or under-specification.

  • Definition of your production requirements, product range, and target capacity as the basis for every equipment decision.
  • Assessment of the available machinery and tooling options, comparing capacity, efficiency, reliability, and cost-effectiveness.
  • Evaluation of the full cost of ownership, including purchase, installation, operation, maintenance, and spare parts.
  • Review of supplier offers and technical specifications to support well-informed purchasing decisions.
  • Alignment of equipment with the factory layout, utilities, safety requirements, and the skills of your workforce.
  • Selection of the toolkits and auxiliary equipment needed to keep production running smoothly.

The result is a clear, justified equipment selection that balances performance and cost, and is matched to your production goals from the start.

Planning

We can help you develop a detailed project plan that outlines timelines, budgets, resource allocation, and risk management strategies to ensure smooth project execution.

A turn-key project involves many connected steps and parties. A clear plan keeps all of them aligned, from the first decision to the first production run, whether you are starting a new project or expanding an existing one.

  • Breakdown of the project into clear stages, activities, and milestones with realistic timelines.
  • Budget preparation and cost control for each phase, so that spending stays visible and under management.
  • Allocation of people, equipment, materials, and other resources across the project.
  • Identification and assessment of risks, with mitigation and contingency strategies prepared in advance.
  • Definition of responsibilities and coordination between your team, suppliers, and contractors.
  • Progress monitoring and reporting, so deviations are detected early and corrected quickly.

The result is a practical roadmap that supports smooth execution, helps you stay on schedule and on budget, and reduces surprises along the way.

Production

Our team can provide guidance and support throughout the production process, from setting up production lines to implementing quality control measures.

Turning a planned factory into an operating one is a critical phase. We work alongside your team to set up production in a structured way and bring it to stable, efficient operation.

  • Setup of production lines, including the sequence of operations, workstations, and material flow.
  • Support during installation, commissioning, and the start of production.
  • Definition of production procedures and work instructions so that operations are consistent and repeatable.
  • Optimization of workflow and process efficiency to reduce waste, delays, and bottlenecks.
  • Implementation of quality control measures within the production process.
  • Guidance for your team during ramp-up, until production runs stably at the planned level.

The result is production lines that are set up properly, operate efficiently, and are ready to deliver your output and quality targets.

Quality Assurance

We prioritize quality at every stage of the project and offer services such as quality audits, inspections, and certifications to ensure that your products meet industry standards and customer expectations.

Quality is not something to check only at the end. We build it into every stage of the project, from planning and equipment through to the finished product, so that standards are met consistently.

  • Quality audits of your processes, facilities, and practices against the relevant industry standards.
  • Inspections at key stages of the project and of production, to detect issues early.
  • Support toward the certifications relevant to your industry and your customers.
  • Identification of gaps, with practical corrective and preventive actions.
  • Quality control procedures and documentation that your team can apply day to day.
  • Guidance on maintaining quality standards over time as production grows.

The result is products that meet industry standards and customer expectations, and a quality system that supports your reputation and growth.
