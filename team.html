Executive Partners

Wael Ziada

Executive Partner

Capital markets expert in the Middle East with more than 25 years of experience. Took part in numerous initial public offerings (IPOs) and held leadership positions at EFG Hermes until 2016. Currently a board member at five financial institutions, including Banque du Caire. Holds a bachelor’s degree in economics from the American University in Cairo.

Omar El-Shenity

Executive Partner

More than 20 years of experience in investment banking and consulting. Co-founded Multiples Group in 2010 and founded Quick-Wins. Previously worked at Booz Allen Hamilton and Henkel. Holds an MBA from Columbia and LBS, a master’s degree in financial law from KCL, and a bachelor’s degree in economics from the American University in Cairo. Serves as an assistant professor at AUC and AAST.

Marwan Younis

Executive Partner

More than 20 years of experience in Middle East capital markets, having raised more than $4 billion. Founding partner and former Chief Investment Officer at Naeem Holding, and has held positions on the boards of several companies. Holds a bachelor’s degree in business administration from the American University in Cairo.

Khaled Gamal

Executive Partner

Founding and managing partner at Zilla Capital with about 25 years of experience in investment banking. Held leadership positions at EFG Hermes and led transactions worth more than $20 billion. Holds a bachelor’s degree in economics from the American University in Cairo.

Mostafa El-Shenity

Executive Partner

Managing partner in the investment bank at Zilla Capital and member of its executive committee. More than 15 years of experience in investment and private equity. Co-founded Multiples Group in 2010. Holds a bachelor’s degree in engineering from Alexandria University and postgraduate degrees from LBS, CASS and the American University in Cairo.

Hisham Afifi

Executive Partner

Expert in strategic and management consulting with more than 20 years of experience across the Middle East and North Africa. Partner and board member in consulting and real estate companies.
